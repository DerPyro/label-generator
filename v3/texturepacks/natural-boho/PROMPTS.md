### Foreground cutout refinement

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied botanical sprig image. Keep the mushroom, flower, leaves, thin stems, decorative curled ribbons and their fine painted texture exactly as illustrated. Remove ALL surrounding background, including the white/gray gradient in the upper half and ALL colored glow/halo between and around plant parts. Every pixel outside the actual botanical silhouette must have alpha zero. Clean true transparent cutout, no white or gray backdrop, no drop shadow, no glows, no paper rectangle. Do not redraw the botanical subject. Deliver transparent PNG.


Built-in Imagegen was used (no API/CLI fallback). All overlays requested `transparent_background: true`; the portrait paper base requested false. The portrait base painting served as a style reference for the other artwork. Each stage is a separate generated PNG, not a recolour or opacity duplicate. No dynamic text was requested in the imagery. Tool output files were copied into the pack; final PNG encoding preserves the RGBA pixel samples.
